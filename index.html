About the academy

A small school with a long memory

Perne (перне) is the Kazakh word for the frets tied along the neck of a dombyra. We chose it because that is how we teach: one small step at a time, each one in tune.

Our students learn the küis of Kurmangazy and Dina Nurpeisova alongside modern repertoire, in groups small enough that every teacher knows every student by name.

  • Heritage first

    Traditional küi, terme and folk song are at the core of every program.

  • Small groups

    Never more than six students per group lesson.

  • On stage, often

    Every student performs at least twice a year.

Questions

Before you enroll

Do I need my own dombyra?

No. For the first three months we lend every student an instrument. After that our teachers help you choose one that fits your hand and budget.

What is the youngest age you accept?

Six years for instruments and singing. Children of five can join the “Little musicians” rhythm-and-song group.

Is it too late to start as an adult?

Never. Our evening group has students from 19 to 67. Most play their first full küi within a year.

Is the trial lesson really free?

Yes — 30 minutes with a teacher, no obligation. Book it with the form below.
